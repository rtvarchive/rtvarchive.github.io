ОркестрКристалл

The ensemble participated in recording TV themes, concert programs, light orchestral pieces and background music for television broadcasts and seasonal programming.

This playlist collects instrumental recordings by the orchestra: calm melodies with warm strings, gentle brass and the characteristic sound of television orchestral music of the era. The compositions reflect the atmosphere of city walks, summer evenings, parks, streets and everyday moments — music that could have accompanied television programs, studio productions and broadcasts of the time.
