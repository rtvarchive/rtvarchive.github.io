Kolosvit Ensemble

Ensemble "Kolosvit" (1970–1974) is a fictional reconstruction of a Ukrainian ensemble inspired by the aesthetics of folk music and the sound of the 1970s. The early recordings were close to traditional folk song, built around acoustic instruments, vocal harmonies and Ukrainian folk melodies, exploring themes of nature, roads, love, and the changing seasons. By the middle of the 1970s, the ensemble’s sound began to change, introducing extended guitar passages, flute, and psychedelic elements, while the folk melodies remained at its core. After 1974, the trail of the ensemble disappears, leaving its later history unknown.
