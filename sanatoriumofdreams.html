СанаторийСнов

They say every city has places that appear only when you are not looking for them. A courtyard that is not on any map. A window where the light is always on. A street you don’t remember walking down.

“Sanatorium of Dreams” is one of those stories. In the summer of 1994, a small cycle of songs appeared about a city where time sometimes slows down, memories live their own lives, and ordinary things quietly become more important than they seem.

Sanatorium of Dreams — a small collection of urban stories unfolding somewhere between memory and reality. Forgotten streets, familiar faces and empty courtyards drift through a world where ordinary life slowly becomes strange, and memories may not always belong to those who remember them.
