Sanatorium of Dreams

Sanatorium of Dreams — a fictional cycle of urban stories unfolding somewhere between memory and reality, created in 1994. They say every city has places that appear only when you are not looking for them: a courtyard that is not on any map, a window where the light is always on, a street you don’t remember walking down. Forgotten streets, familiar faces and empty courtyards drift through a world where ordinary life slowly becomes strange, and memories may not always belong to those who remember them.
