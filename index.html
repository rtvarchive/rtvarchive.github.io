Section 01 · The Archive

A label built from fragments

About archive →

RADIUS ARCHIVE grew out of the archives of the RADIUS Television Center — a broadcasting facility in the closed scientific city of Radinsk that operated between 1976 and 1998. Internal broadcasts, music programs, documentaries and late-night transmissions survived only in fragments: magnetic tapes, cassettes, photographs, and the occasional document. Each release on this label is a reconstruction of one of those fragments — a record that may have existed somewhere between history, memory and an alternative reality.
