RADIUS ARCHIVE / Projects

Fifteen Projects. One Catalogue.

RADIUS ARCHIVE is an independent label grown from the archives of the RADIUS Television Center (Radinsk, 1976–1998). Each project is a release from the same world — a forgotten tape, a lost transmission, or a record that might have existed somewhere between history, memory, and an alternative reality.

The catalogue spans across decades and genres, from the raw post-punk of the 1980s underground to the synthetic waves of the late Soviet era, and into the independent scenes of the 1990s and beyond. Every release is carefully reconstructed from surviving fragments, preserving the hiss, the wear, and the atmosphere of the original media. These are not just recordings; they are artifacts of a parallel history, waiting to be discovered again.

Synthpop · Coldwave

AVAILABLE / LISTEN

(ФАЗА/НОЛЬ) — recovered recordings attributed to the fictional Institute of Silence.
