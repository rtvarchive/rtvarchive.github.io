RADIUS ARCHIVE /Projets

Eight Projects. One Archive.

RADIUS ARCHIVE is a fictional archive of lost broadcasts, underground recordings, and reconstructed musical documents. Each project is a fragment of a larger story — a forgotten tape, a missing transmission, or a record from a world that might have existed somewhere between history, memory, and an alternative reality.

605 Микрорайон

Post-punk · Alternative Rock

DISTRIBUTED

605 Mikrorayon — lost post-punk and coldwave recordings from a forgotten urban archive. Cassette hiss, rehearsal rooms, empty courtyards and fragments of everyday life preserve the memory of a city that may no longer exist.

10 singles → Archival Recordings · 1986—1993

ФАЗА/НОЛЬ

Synthpop · Coldwave

DISTRIBUTED

PHASE/ZERO — recovered recordings attributed to the fictional Institute of Silence. Synthpop, coldwave and Sovietwave textures emerge through magnetic tape degradation, laboratory signals and fragments of a research archive that was never meant to be found.

6 singles → Archival Recordings · 1989—1991
P · 03

Ритуал-84

Ambient · Ethnic electronica

DISTRIBUTED

Ritual-84 — reconstructed field recordings from expeditions that never officially took place. Northern landscapes, ritual fragments, early electronics and damaged magnetic tape preserve traces of journeys that exist somewhere between folklore, memory and archival fiction.

1 album → summer expedition, 84
P · 04

Радиус-78

Broadcast · Lost Media

YouTube + Audius

RADIUS-78 — a fictional collection of broadcasts from the RADIUS Television Center, spanning the late 1970s through the final years of the archive. Test signals, announcer recordings, music programs and lost transmissions reconstruct fragments of an alternative broadcast history.

5 singles → BROADCAST FILES · 1978—1998

Оркестр Кристалл

Classical · Orchestral

YouTube + Audius

Crystal Orchestra — the reconstructed recordings of a fictional television studio orchestra active during the late 1970s. Orchestral themes, concert arrangements and background music preserve the sound of a vanished production studio and the broadcasts it helped bring to life.

4 singles → SELECTED RECORDINGS

Санаторий Снов

Dream Pop · Soft Rock

YouTube + Audius

Sanatorium of Dreams — a small collection of urban stories unfolding somewhere between memory and reality. Forgotten streets, familiar faces and empty courtyards drift through a world where ordinary life slowly becomes strange, and memories may not always belong to those who remember them.

4 singles → RECOVERED DREAMS · MAGIC REALISM

Дикий Мёд

Folk Noir · Gothic Folk

YouTube + Audius

Wild Honey — a cycle of rural legends collected as fragments of oral memory. Folk-noir songs, whispered stories and distant voices preserve tales that lived between people, changing shape each time they were remembered and retold.

3 singles → FOLK NOIR · RURAL ARCHIVE

Soundtrack · Television Theatre

SOON
