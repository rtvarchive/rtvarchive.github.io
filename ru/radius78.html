Most recordings survived on reel-to-reel magnetic tapes with varying degrees of deterioration. Some materials have been reconstructed from incomplete fragments and are accompanied by only partial technical documentation.

According to the surviving records, the RADIUS Television Center served as a regional broadcasting facility while also participating in a number of experimental projects involving broadcast design, signal transmission, and television audio production.

RADIUS-78 — a fictional collection of broadcasts from the RADIUS Television Center, spanning the late 1970s through the final years of the archive. Test signals, announcer recordings, music programs and lost transmissions reconstruct fragments of an alternative broadcast history.
