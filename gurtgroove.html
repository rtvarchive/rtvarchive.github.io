Gurt Groove

Gurt Groove was a Ukrainian music group formed in 1998. The group emerged during a period when Ukrainian musicians were increasingly combining different rhythmic and electronic styles, experimenting with sampling, live instrumentation and new approaches to beat-driven music.

The musical foundation of Groove was built around funk and breakbeat. Their tracks combined funky break patterns, bass lines, instrumental riffs, samples and electronic elements. Alternative instrumental hip-hop was another important part of the group’s sound, without relying on a traditional rap-oriented song structure or making vocals the central element.

The group worked at the intersection of several contemporary scenes. Its music drew from club breakbeat, funk, funky breaks, instrumental hip-hop and electronic music of the late 1990s. Rather than following one specific genre formula, Groove built its tracks around rhythm, bass, repetition and movement, combining live instruments with electronic production techniques.

The main period of activity ran from 1998 into the early 2000s. During this time, the group developed a recognizable approach based on energetic rhythms, short instrumental phrases, repeating motifs and gradual changes within a track.

2001 is used as the main archival reference point for the project. This period represents the characteristic combination of funk, breakbeat, funky breaks and alternative instrumental hip-hop associated with Gurt Groove. Within the reconstruction, 2001 serves as the chronological reference for the group’s music and visual identity.

The musical language of Gurt Groove is built around funky bass lines, breakbeat and funky breaks, live drums and percussion, instrumental riffs, sampling, electronic textures, hip-hop-oriented rhythmic production and predominantly instrumental arrangements. The central element is the groove itself — a rhythmic pattern around which the composition develops.
