VIA Vanity Fair

VIA "Vanity Fair" (1976–1979) is an artistic reconstruction of a fictional ensemble whose origins cannot be conclusively verified. According to surviving references, the group was attached to a regional philharmonic society and combined chamber orchestra, acoustic rock, literary songwriting, and touches of baroque pop. Its songs told small stories about people, time, memory, and choice, while the name "Vanity Fair" was a metaphor for a world where everyone plays a role, searches for something, loses something, and passes by the lives of others. Following the release of their only album, the project was reportedly discontinued due to a "lack of broad public interest," and the master tapes were believed to have been lost.
