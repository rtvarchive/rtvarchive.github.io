ВИА Восточный Базар

VIA “Oriental Bazaar” — “Star Road” (1984)

VIA “Oriental Bazaar” was a Soviet vocal-instrumental ensemble active on the concert circuit during the first half of the 1980s. In 1984, the group prepared the program “Star Road”, combining Soviet estrada songs with Eastern and folk-inspired motifs.

In 2026, a magnetic recording of the program, originally prepared by the ensemble in 1984, was discovered in a private archive. The recording had never been officially released.
