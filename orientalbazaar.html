VIA Oriental Bazaar

VIA "Oriental Bazaar" was a fictional Soviet vocal-instrumental ensemble active on the concert circuit during the first half of the 1980s. In 1984, the group prepared the program "Star Road", combining Soviet estrada songs with Eastern and folk-inspired motifs. The musical material is built on a blend of psychedelic rock, folk, and space-disco, creating a unique sound that mixes electric guitars, synthesizers, and traditional Eastern instruments, reflecting the spirit of the era and the atmosphere of a never-ending musical journey.
