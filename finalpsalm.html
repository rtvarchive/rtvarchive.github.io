Final Psalm

Final Psalm is an independent Ukrainian music project by Dan, born at the intersection of acoustic reggae, personal songwriting, and spiritual reflection.

In 2013, the project released its album While the Fire Still Burns — a musical journey through human connection, loss, hope, inner freedom, and the light that continues to shine even in the darkest moments.

The sound is built around a warm male baritone, acoustic guitar, a gentle reggae rhythm, deep bass, and subtle atmospheric textures. Intimate and organic, the music leaves room for words, silence, and the listener’s own emotions.

The project’s name refers to Psalm 150, the final psalm in the Book of Psalms, known by its Latin incipit, Laudate Dominum in sanctis eius. It is a call to praise God through music and dance, naming musical instruments in celebration. For this reason, Psalm 150 is also known as the “Psalm of the Musicians.”

For the project, this image brings together the spiritual and the human, personal experience and universal questions about life, faith, love, and the limits of our existence.

While the fire still burns, warmth remains within us. Music continues to sound. And we remain capable of feeling and hoping.

Welcome to the world of Final Psalm.
